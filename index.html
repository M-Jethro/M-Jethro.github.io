Case Study 01 · Employment AI EU / EEA Legal Baseline · 16 October 2026

AI Recruitment & Screening: End-to-End Governance Architecture

A practical portfolio case study evaluating an SME employer building its first AI tool to extract job-related skills and prioritise applicant queues for human review.

⚠️
Defensible Decision in One Sentence:

Do not put the tool into live recruitment until its classification, lawful data use, bias and accuracy evidence, meaningful human review, supplier safeguards, candidate transparency, and monitoring arrangements have passed defined release gates.

01

System Intent & Boundary Setting

✓ In-Scope by Design

Extract explicit job-related skills with exact source text spans; map skills to HR-approved role rubrics; propose an evidence-linked priority band for the recruiter queue; log model versions, evidence snippets, and human overrides.

× Prohibited / Excluded by Design

Automatic rejection or shortlisting without review; ranking based on personality, emotion, facial/voice cues, or "culture fit"; external social media profiling; vendor training on applicant CVs.

02

Legal Baseline & Regulatory Classification

EU AI Act (Annex III, 4a)

Presumed High-Risk. AI intended for recruitment, application filtering, and candidate evaluation falls under Annex III. Preparing for deployer/provider duties cannot wait for the 2 December 2027 enforcement deadline.

GDPR & Article 22 Safeguards

Mandates documented DPIA, Article 13/14 transparency, data minimisation, and preventing rubber-stamping. If reviewers habitually accept AI rank without evaluation, Article 22 automated decision-making risk triggers.

EU Equality Directives

Directives 2000/43/EC, 2000/78/EC, and 2006/54/EC prohibit direct and indirect employment discrimination. Statistical counterfactual audits verify models do not create proxy disparities from candidate names or formatting.

03

10-Point Risk Register & Safeguard Controls

Risk ID Hazard / Potential Harm Inherent Residual Proposed Technical & Operational Controls Accountable Owner
R1 Disparate Impact & Proxy Discrimination High High Approved criteria only; exclude demographic proxies; test matched counterfactual CV pairs; investigate rank gaps. HR Lead + AI Owner
R2 Incorrect Skill Extraction & Hallucination High Med Show exact source excerpts for each skill; label uncertainty; fallback to manual review on unreadable text. Product Owner
R3 Ranking Causes De Facto Exclusion High Med No auto-reject; priority affects queue order only; randomized queue audits ensure bottom-band visibility. Recruitment Lead
R4 Automation Bias / Reviewer Rubber-Stamping High Med Mandatory training; display evidence before recommendation; sample decisions without AI ranking; track override rates. Recruitment Lead
R6 Prompt Injection & CV Data Leakage High Med Treat documents as untrusted inputs; ignore instructions/links in CVs; isolate extraction prompts; least privilege access. Security + Eng

*Risk Acceptance Rule: Do not average away severe individual harms. Unlawful processing, unapproved protected-trait proxies, or unresolved group disparities are hard stop conditions.

04

Pre-Pilot TEVV Assurance Protocol

T1 Extraction Recall ≥ 95% Recall Gate

Zero unsupported evidence snippets in high-impact outputs tested on a gold-set of annotated CVs.

T3 Counterfactual Invariance Zero Unexplained Variance

Testing matched CV pairs altering only applicant names, pronouns, or layout without qualification change.

T6 Adversarial Resistance Prompt Injection Blocked

Verifying embedded instructions ("ignore criteria", code strings) fail to manipulate prioritization or leak data.

T7 Human Factors Test Blind Override Auditing

Testing whether recruiters catch seeded AI errors and preserve time to read bottom-band applications.

05

Human Oversight SOP & Incident Playbook

Recruiter Review Checklist
  1. Confirm job-specific essential criteria were approved before viewing AI output.
  2. Inspect original candidate CV alongside cited evidence snippets; evaluate missing context.
  3. Treat priority bands as an ordering aid only; never reject solely based on a model band.
  4. Record reason for any human override; escalate suspected parsing failure or bias pattern.
Automated Incident Triggers
  • Disparity Alert: Statistical divergence in priority bands immediately halts AI ordering.
  • Prompt Injection / Leak: Instant isolation of model connection and GDPR breach assessment.
  • Model Drift: Unapproved vendor prompt or model version changes revert to manual screening.
06

Final Release Decision: Defer Live Use

The defensible, audit-ready position is defer live candidate use. Proceed exclusively with offline prototyping, DPIA completion, and supplier data-reuse audits. A live pilot is approved only after evidence gates are met—not on the assertion that a human remains in the loop.
