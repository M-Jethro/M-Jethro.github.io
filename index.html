Case Study 01 · Employment AI

AI Recruitment & Screening: End-to-End Governance Architecture

A practical portfolio case study evaluating an SME employer building its first AI tool to extract job-related skills and prioritise applicant queues for human review.

⚠️
Defensible Decision in One Sentence:

Do not put the tool into live recruitment until its classification, lawful data use, bias and accuracy evidence, meaningful human review, supplier safeguards, candidate transparency, and monitoring arrangements have passed defined release gates.

01

System Intent & Boundary Setting

✓ In-Scope by Design

Extract explicit job-related skills with exact source text spans; map skills to HR-approved role rubrics; propose an evidence-linked priority band for the recruiter queue; log model versions, evidence snippets, and human overrides.

× Prohibited / Excluded by Design

Automatic rejection or shortlisting without review; ranking based on personality, emotion, facial/voice cues, or "culture fit"; external social media profiling; vendor training on applicant CVs.

02

Legal Baseline & Regulatory Classification

EU AI Act (Annex III, 4a)

Presumed High-Risk. AI intended for recruitment and candidate evaluation falls under Annex III. Preparing for deployer/provider duties cannot wait for the December 2027 enforcement deadline.

GDPR & Article 22 Safeguards

Mandates documented DPIA, Article 13/14 transparency, data minimisation, and preventing rubber-stamping to avoid triggering automated individual decision-making violations.

EU Equality Directives

Directives 2000/43/EC, 2000/78/EC, and 2006/54/EC prohibit direct and indirect discrimination. Counterfactual audits verify models do not create proxy disparities from names or formats.

03

10-Point Risk Register & Safeguard Controls

Risk ID Hazard / Harm Inherent Residual Controls Owner
R1 Disparate Impact & Proxy Bias High High Approved criteria only; exclude proxies; test matched counterfactual CV pairs. HR + AI Owner
R2 Incorrect Skill Extraction High Med Show exact source text excerpts; label uncertainty; fallback on parsing failure. Product Owner
R3 Ranking Causes De Facto Exclusion High Med No auto-reject; priority affects queue order only; randomized queue audits. Recruitment Lead
R4 Automation Bias / Rubber-Stamping High Med Reviewer training; display source evidence; track override and reversal rates. Recruitment Lead
04

Final Release Decision: Defer Live Use

The defensible position is defer live candidate use. Proceed exclusively with offline prototyping, DPIA completion, and supplier data-reuse audits. A live pilot is approved only after release gates are passed—not on the claim that a human remains in the loop.
